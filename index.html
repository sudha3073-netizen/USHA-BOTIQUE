TRANSPARENT POLICY

Important Terms & Conditions

1

Stitching Charges Only

All prices listed on the card and menu represent labour / stitching charges only for tailoring the requested pattern.

2

Materials & Accessories Extra

Base fabric, inner lining, borders/lace, padded cups, designer buttons, zippers, dori & latkan are charged extra as per usage.

3

Heavy Work Charged Separately

Heavy zardozi, hand embroidery, sequin work, maggam work, cutwork and stone setting are charged separately based on intricacy.

4

Urgent Delivery Charges

Express 24 to 48-hour delivery requests may attract urgent surcharge depending on workshop queue and artisan availability.

5

Final Price Confirmed After Design Consultation

Every client's measurements, fabric weight, neck-depth, and custom flair are unique. The final price is confirmed with mutual agreement right after measuring and checking the customer's garment sketch or reference image.
